TO THE GOOD HEED OF THY DISCERNING AND ILLUSTRIOUS READER
=========================================================

On the forging of 'IcoWM':

Hearken!  Behold this programme!  'Tis forged with stalwart `vi` (`nvi`
upon the one and eightieth iteration, and six more added thereto, of its
first form, no less (1.81.6), and e'en so oft `vim` upon its ninth
(9.0), cloaked in compatible guise, shouldst thou yearn for accuracy)
and wrought upon the stout GNU/Linux; 'tis true; yet 'midst this, my
boundless and undying affection for each and ev'ry manifold iteration of
BSD doth ever endure.  'Twas uttered forth upon the mighty, magnificent
portable Sony Vaio TZ[21WN/B], mine unwavering companion since nigh the
year two thousand and seven.  A wayfarer through realms both near and
distant, and it didst witness my woes and tribulations, as I clasped
close the haunting specter of sleepless nights, seeking solace in the
warm embrace of ciphered lore.  For this very programme, the hours
twenty and four, broken asunder 'neath a dozen non-sequential suns, in
the measure of thrice and half hours *per diem*, were perchance as
offerings to the sacred art of creation of this first draft begetting,
whereupon each keystroke on its faithful keys didst become a whisper in
the dead of night, each line, a tremor, a reverberation of my restless
spirit, weaving Time's tapestry in tranquil musing and silent reverie.
I craft, therefore I evolve.

> Verily, 'tis not the province of us, the noble artificers of code, to
> plumb the depths of woe wherein users, systems, and the very lines we
> weave do languish, be it through fault or Fortune's cruel jest.  Nay,
> our duty lieth not in judgment, but in succour.  Our eyes must be
> fixed solely upon their present plight, to lend aid in their hour of
> need, and not to rake o'er the ashes of errors past.

Ev'ry programmer, a Quixote, doth strive with fervent might 'gainst the
giants that loom in the realm of code.  The sole tongue that
a programmer doth fully know is that of lamentation and of striving.

In yon realm of fainèant scribes and digital quills, where desks oft do
whisper of labor's call, the intention hath ever been, and ever shall
be, to forge a sanctuary of productivity, wherein the scriber of code's
spirit may flourish unencumbered.  The icons doth stand as steadfast
sentinels, guardians of thought, representing not mere distractions, but
rather the delicate minimisations of that which doth continue to be
unfolded in the recesses of the mind.  With purpose pure, each emblem
doth symbolize a task half-formed, awaiting patiently to be realised,
whilst the laborer remaineth anchored in the placid quietude of
creation.  No gaze dost lift unto the ever-changing tapestry of the
task-bar, for the true wisdom lieth not in fleeting glances, but rather
in the focused intent of the work at hand.

Methinks that in the nascent hour of these new-sprung pictur'd
interfaces of userkind, by shrinking a casement, 'twas transform'd into
a sigil, nimble and fair to behold, sign and device of image fine, where
purpose and comeliness didst intertwine.  Thus doth it allow the user to
attend to divers tasks, preserving a visual tether to their labours, and
aiding both in remembrance and navigation upon the digital seas of data,
this veritable ocean of knowledge.  Pixelated tokens of salvation, or
perchance, symbols of redemption's grace limned in favour, bound to the
present toil in progress.

Thus, as TWM did weave this cherished ideal, so too did his legacy
inspire a few who sought to reclaim this treasured custom long lost
amidst the din of restless panels and the clamor of system trays.  In
this hallowed space, where organisation doth find harmony and creativity
reigneth, we endeavor to restore that which time hath shrouded in
complexity, revitalizing the craft of writing the writ of the origins as
a noble art, serene and untroubled.  The script of creation, transform'd
into the craft of manifold instructions, beginneth with curious eyes
glancing upon a fanciful desktop that converteth thought into action,
devoid of distractions.  Hence, the iconification upon a blank canvas
mayhap it be the answer.

In sooth, 'tis the deepest wish and hope of mine own heart that this
selfsame programme shall serve thee with good fortune's grace, and
favour and prosperous happestance.

Fare thee well now, esteemed programmers & artisans of digital loom!
May the muses of the algorithms ever guide thy nimble fingers upon the
keys, and the syntax of thy craft shine forth, ever clear and true.  As
thou dost traverse the vast realms of code, may thy logic be sound,
a hammered shilling, and thy creativity know no bounds.  'Til we do once
more convene amidst the lines of code, I bid thee farewell, with hopes
for prosperous ventures in thy endeavors and wishing for Fortune's
favour upon thine undertakings.  Together, let us weave the tapestry of
code and craft wonders that shall echo through the chronicles of
technology's age.

*jaj QaQ*!
